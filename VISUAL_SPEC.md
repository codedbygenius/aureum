# Aureum Hero Visual Specification

## Reference Analysis

- Composition: full-bleed 16:9 cinematic plate, low-angle statue centered slightly right, vast serif title spanning nearly the full width, brand/navigation as small precision marks around the image.
- Grid behavior: asymmetric editorial frame with wide horizontal title band, left marginal vertical copy, right marginal date mark, top brand/nav row, bottom collection/CTA row.
- Focal points: statue face and torso, halo/ring behind head, oversized `ASCEND` letterforms, warm backlight from upper center.
- Hierarchy: image first, title second as translucent monumental overlay, then brand/nav/CTA as fine jewelry-like interface marks.
- Typography: high-contrast Didot/Bodoni-style serif for the title, light uppercase sans for navigation and metadata, italic old-style serif for the poetic subcopy.
- Spacing rhythm: generous edge padding, tiny metadata pushed to edges, title occupying the central third, CTA anchored low-right.
- Cinematic balance: dark vignette edges, luminous gold center, marble/cloth textures kept visible under a soft atmospheric veil.
- Negative space: smoky left sky and upper right shadow preserve breathing room for vertical text and nav.
- Depth layering: clean plate at rear, mist/light overlays, title blended above image, UI marks above all.
- Overlay treatment: radial glow, side vignettes, subtle grain, restrained glass blur only on CTA.
- Palette: black-green shadows, charcoal smoke, ivory marble, champagne gold, antique brass, warm cloud highlights.
- Texture/material: marble veins, worn metallic gold, fabric folds, dusty air, soft lens bloom.
- Crop logic: desktop keeps statue at center-right and title full-width; tablet shifts background right; mobile crops toward statue/halo and simplifies marginal marks.
- Motion: micro parallax on background and title highlight only; hover interactions are quiet and linear-to-eased.
- Tone: mythic, immortal, imperial, ceremonial, expensive, art-campaign rather than software product.
- Luxury positioning: minimal copy, exacting letter spacing, ornamental marks, restrained CTA, high-contrast serif scale.

## Extracted System

- Font styles: Didot/Bodoni serif display, uppercase geometric sans metadata, italic literary serif supporting line.
- Weights: display 400, metadata 400/500 feel through letter spacing, no bold UI.
- Letter spacing: very wide tracking for nav/metadata, slight negative tracking only on display title.
- Section spacing: clamp-based frame padding from 20px mobile to 48px desktop; elements anchored to viewport edges.
- Button language: pill outline, transparent glass tint, plus icon, thin border, uppercase tracking.
- Navigation language: small uppercase links with hairline underline on hover.
- Border radius: circular sigils and full-pill CTA; otherwise no cards.
- Shadow philosophy: image-driven depth and vignettes, not component shadows.
- Animation philosophy: subtle, slow, physically quiet; no large entrance choreography.
